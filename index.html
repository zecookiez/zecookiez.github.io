Hi, I'm Zeyu Chen.


I am a high school student who enjoys playing around with programming. One of my favorite past-times is to solve logic puzzles in efficient and creative manners.

I began learning how to code since I was 14, when I started my first project of a centimeter to meter units conversion tool. What started as a small school project began a growing passion, and soon I was using code to solve some everyday problems.

My fascination to be able to share my experiences with others, as well as the power to make anything drives my motivation to always keep learning and experimenting.
